# Positions

A [position](../../glossary.md#position) defines the role a [player](../players/index.md) occupies on their [team](../teams/index.md).

- A position has a name.
- A position is available to one or more [race eras](../races/index.md) (a
  [race](../races/index.md) within a specific [era](../eras/index.md)), not to
  races directly — availability can change from one era to the next.
  Positions are available to a single race across its eras; a
  [star player](../star-players/index.md) is the exception.
- Only players whose team era belongs to one of the position's race eras may
  hold a given position.
