# Positions

A [position](../../glossary.md#position) defines the role a [player](../players/index.md) occupies on their [team](../teams/index.md).

- A position has a name.
- A position can play for one or more [races](../races/index.md). Most positions
  play for a single race; a star player is a single position available to several
  races.
- Each position-race relation may be current or historical: a relation is marked
  deleted when the position was only ever available under a race that has since
  been removed, so it is kept for history rather than offered going forward.
- Only players whose team belongs to one of the position's current (non-deleted)
  races may hold a given position.
