# Team Eras

A [team era](../../glossary.md#team-era) is a [team](../teams/index.md)'s membership within a single [era](../eras/index.md) — the unit that owns everything reset when a team is rebooted into a new era.

- A team era belongs to exactly one [team](../teams/index.md) and exactly one [era](../eras/index.md).
- A team may have at most one team era per era.
- A team's [players](../players/index.md) belong to exactly one team era, not to the team directly — a player's roster resets to empty each time a team enters a new era.
- [Competition](../competitions/index.md) enrollment and [match](../matches/index.md) participation happen through a team's team era for that era, not through the team directly.
