# Teams

A [team](../../glossary.md#team) is the core playing entity: a named group of players of a single race, managed by one coach.

- A team has a name.
- A team belongs to exactly one [race](../races/index.md).
- A team has exactly one [coach](../coaches/index.md).
- A team has zero or more [players](../players/index.md) on its roster.
- A team may be enrolled in any number of [competitions](../competitions/index.md) over its lifetime, but not more than one competition at a time within a [league](../leagues/index.md).
